Frequently Asked Questions & Customer Support

Q: How do I sync my parking spot with my Wear OS watch?
Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on both your phone and watch. When you pin a spot on either device, ParkPin automatically synchronizes your active parking location, vehicle details, and duration timer in real-time over the Wearable Data Layer API.
Q: How do I add ParkPin Tiles or Complications on Wear OS?
Swipe left on your watch face to reach Tiles, tap and hold, then tap "+" to add the ParkPin Parking Tile. To add a watch face complication, customize your active watch face, select a slot, and choose ParkPin Status or Duration.
Q: How do Home Screen Widgets work?
Long press on your Android home screen, select "Widgets", scroll to ParkPin, and pick from Compact (2x2), Standard (4x2), or Dashboard (4x3) layouts.
Q: Does ParkPin store my location history online?
No. All parking coordinates and history are stored locally on your device for complete privacy and security.
Q: How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
As long as you are logged into the same Google Play account used to purchase ParkPin Premium, your entitlement is automatically restored upon launching the app.
